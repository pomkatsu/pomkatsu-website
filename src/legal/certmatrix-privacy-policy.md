## Introduction

Pomkatsu LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Pomkatsu," "we," "us," or "our"), runs CertMatrix, a service that helps businesses keep track of their team's certifications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle, why, who we share it with and how long we keep it. It covers:

- The website at [certmatrix.io](https://certmatrix.io)
- The CertMatrix app, its upload links and its emails
- Emails we send to business contacts about CertMatrix

Pomkatsu's mobile apps have their own policy at [pomkatsu.com/privacy](/privacy).

This policy should be read with the CertMatrix [Terms of Service](/apps/certmatrix/terms) and [Data Processing Addendum](/apps/certmatrix/dpa).

## Two Roles, in Plain Terms

We handle information in two different ways.

**When we decide how information is used.** This covers people who sign in to CertMatrix, people who visit our website, people who pay for a plan, and business contacts we email. For this information we are the "controller."

**When our customer decides.** A business that uses CertMatrix keeps records about its own employees and contractors. That business decides what goes in and what happens to it. We only handle those records on its behalf, as its "processor," under our Data Processing Addendum.

> **If your employer uses CertMatrix,** your employer is responsible for your record. To see it, correct it or have it removed, ask your employer. We will help them do it.

## Information About Account Holders and Visitors

### What You Give Us

- **Your sign-in email address.** We do not ask for your name, and there are no passwords
- **Your organization's details:** its name, its timezone and your role in it
- **Messages you send us,** such as support requests

### What We Record When You Use the App

- **An audit log of actions** taken in your organization. Each entry has the date, what was done, and the IP address and browser details of the device that did it
- **A record that you agreed to our Terms:** the version, the date and time, your IP address and your browser details
- **Server logs** of requests to our systems, kept by our hosting provider for a short rolling period
- **Usage and error reports.** These are counts, timings and error types tied to a random account ID and organization ID. They do not include your email, names, documents or anything you type. We do not record your screen

### What We Get From Our Payment Provider

When you subscribe, Lemon Squeezy tells us your customer ID, your subscription ID, your plan and its status. We never see or store your card number.

### Website Visitors

The certmatrix.io website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies. Our hosting provider logs requests in the usual way.

## Information We Handle for Our Customers

A customer may put the following into CertMatrix about its team members:

- First and last name
- Email address, if the customer adds one
- An employee or payroll reference number, if the customer adds one
- Supervisor, and whether the person is active or has left
- Certification details: title, issuer, certificate number, issue date and expiry date
- Photos and PDFs of cards and certificates. A photo shows whatever is printed on the card, which can include a picture of the person, a date of birth or a license number
- Spreadsheets the customer imports

If a team member uses an upload link, we record the IP address and browser details of the device they used, in the customer's audit log.

We do not ask for phone numbers or home addresses. CertMatrix is not meant for medical records. See "Health Information" below.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

- Run the Service: sign you in, show your records, send reminders and build exports
- Read uploaded photos and PDFs so that details can be suggested
- Manage subscriptions and answer support requests
- Keep the Service secure, investigate problems and keep an audit trail
- Understand, in aggregate, how the Service is used so that we can improve it
- Meet our legal obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for targeted advertising. We do not use customer documents to train AI models.

CertMatrix does not make decisions about people. It shows dates and statuses to the customer, who decides what to do.

## AI Processing by OpenAI

CertMatrix uses OpenAI to read documents. Here is exactly what happens.

**What we send.** When a photo or PDF is uploaded, we send that file to OpenAI with a fixed set of instructions. We do not send the team roster, the organization's name, account details or the file name. Matching the result to a person happens afterward on our own systems.

When a customer imports a spreadsheet, we may also send the column headings, up to five sample rows and the customer's requirement names, so that the columns can be matched.

**What comes back.** Suggested details: name, title, issuer, dates and certificate number. A person at the customer's company confirms or corrects them. In one case CertMatrix files a result automatically: a clean upload made through a team member's own upload link. That filing is recorded and can be undone.

**What OpenAI keeps.**

- OpenAI states that it does not use data sent through its API to train its models unless a customer opts in. We do not opt in
- OpenAI may keep what we send, and what it returns, for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse
- Our requests turn off OpenAI's optional storage of responses
- **We do not have OpenAI's "zero data retention" arrangement.** If that changes, we will update this page

> **A photo is sent as soon as it is uploaded.** Anything printed on it reaches OpenAI before a person at the customer's company can review it. Do not upload documents that show Social Security numbers or health details.

- **Supabase**: database, sign-in and file storage. United States. Sign-in codes are sent through Supabase's sign-in service
- **Render**: hosting for the app. United States. Traffic passes through Cloudflare's network on its way to Render
- **Postmark**: sending email, such as reminders, invitations and notices. United States

- **PostHog**: usage and error reports. United States
- **Lemon Squeezy**: checkout, subscriptions, tax and receipts. Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and handles your payment details under its own privacy policy

We also share information:

- **With the customer's own users.** Owners, admins and viewers of an organization can see that organization's records
- **When the law requires it,** or to protect our rights, our customers or the public
- **If the business changes hands.** If CertMatrix or Pomkatsu is merged or sold, information may pass to the new owner, who must honor this policy
- **With your consent**

## Emails We Send

**Service emails.** Sign-in codes, invitations, reminders, the weekly summary and export notices are part of the Service. They have no marketing content and no unsubscribe link. An owner or admin can change or turn off reminders in the organization's settings.

**Emails to team members.** Reminder and upload-link emails go to team members on behalf of their employer. Replies go to an owner or admin at that employer. If you are a team member and want these emails to stop, ask your employer.

**Tracking.** We do not track opens or clicks in service emails.

**Bounces and complaints.** If an address bounces repeatedly, or the recipient marks our email as spam, we stop sending to that address.

## Business Contacts

We sometimes email people at companies that we think could use CertMatrix.

- **What we keep:** your name, job title, company, business email address and the public page where we found it
- **Where it comes from:** public sources such as company websites and public business listings
- **What we use it for:** only to contact you about CertMatrix
- **Where the email comes from:** addresses at getcertmatrix.com, sent through an email sending provider
- **How to stop it:** every email tells you how to opt out. We act on opt-outs within 10 business days, and usually much sooner. We keep a record of your opt-out so that we do not email you again
- **What we never do:** sell or share this information

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email support@pomkatsu.com.

## Cookies and Local Storage

- **The website** sets no cookies. It remembers your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage
- **The app** uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings, such as theme, text size and which organization you last opened. The app needs this to work
- **Usage and error reports** may store a random identifier in your browser so that reports from the same account can be grouped
- **No advertising cookies** and no tracking across other websites. Because we do not track you across sites, "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals do not change what we do

## How Long We Keep Information

- **Customer records and files:** for as long as the organization exists
- **Files a customer deletes:** they can be restored for 30 days. After that they no longer appear in the app. They are erased from storage when the organization is closed, or sooner if the owner asks us
- **A closed organization:** the owner has 30 days to reopen it. After that we permanently delete its files and records
- **An inactive plan:** we keep the data so that the customer can come back. The Terms explain when we may delete it
- **Export files:** deleted 30 days after they are made
- **The audit log:** kept after an organization is deleted. It holds IDs, dates, statuses, IP addresses, browser details and the organization's name. It does not hold team member names or documents. We keep it for security, to resolve disputes and to meet legal obligations
- **Your sign-in email address:** until you ask us to delete it
- **Your agreement to the Terms:** kept as a record of the agreement
- **Backups:** our database provider keeps backup copies for a short period, then they expire. Deleted information can remain in a backup until then
- **Server logs:** a short rolling period
- **Business contacts:** until you opt out or ask us to delete your details. We keep the opt-out record

**Erasing one person.** An owner can ask us to erase a team member. We remove the person's name, email, reference number, certificate numbers and titles, and their files. Some traces can remain, for example in a spreadsheet the customer imported earlier or in exports the customer already downloaded.

## How We Protect Information

- All connections to CertMatrix are encrypted in transit
- Sign-in uses a one-time code sent by email. We store no passwords
- Each organization's data is kept separate. Every request is checked against the signed-in person's membership, and the database enforces a second barrier of its own
- Uploaded files are kept in private storage. Links to view them expire after five minutes
- Upload links use long random tokens that we store only in hashed form. They expire and can be turned off
- The audit log cannot be edited or deleted, by anyone, through the app
- The database is backed up daily

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects a customer's data, we will tell that customer without undue delay.
