## 2. Definitions

- **"Customer Personal Data"**: personal data in the records, photos, PDFs and spreadsheets that you, your users or your team members put into CertMatrix
- **"Data Protection Law"**: the privacy and data protection laws of the United States and its states that apply to the handling of Customer Personal Data, including the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws
- **"Controller"** and **"Processor"**: also mean "business" and "service provider," or the equivalent terms, where a Data Protection Law uses those words
- **"Subprocessor"**: another company we use to handle Customer Personal Data
- **"Security Incident"**: a breach of our security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration or disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data

Words not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms.

## 3. Roles

For Customer Personal Data, you are the Controller and we are your Processor.

**Your responsibilities.** You decide what goes into CertMatrix and why. You are responsible for:

- Having the right to collect and use the personal data you put in
- Telling your team members that you keep their certification records in CertMatrix, and that photos of their cards are read by an AI provider
- Keeping the data accurate
- Not uploading the data described in Section 14

**Information we control.** We are a Controller in our own right for a small amount of information: account holders' sign-in emails, billing records, audit logs and our business contacts. Our [Privacy Policy](/apps/certmatrix/privacy) covers that information. This DPA does not.

CertMatrix is built for certification, license and training records. Do not upload:

- Bank account or payment card numbers

We are not a business associate under HIPAA and we do not sign business associate agreements.

## 15. AI Processing

- We send the file itself with fixed instructions. We do not send your roster, your organization's name or account details with it

- OpenAI may keep what we send, and what it returns, for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse
- We do not have OpenAI's "zero data retention" arrangement. If that changes, we will update this page and our Privacy Policy

## Annex 2: Security Measures

**Access**
- All connections to the Service are encrypted in transit
- Sign-in uses a one-time code sent by email. No passwords are stored
- Three roles (owner, admin, viewer) limit what each user can do

**Separation of customers**
- Every record belongs to one organization
- All access goes through our application, which limits each request to the organization the signed-in person belongs to. That membership is decided on our servers, never taken from the browser
- The database has row-level security enabled on every table as a second barrier, denying access by default

**Files**
- Uploaded files are kept in private storage
- Links to view or download a file expire after five minutes

**Upload links**
- Upload links use long random tokens that are stored only in hashed form
- They expire, they are limited in how many uploads they accept, and they can be turned off at any time

**Record keeping**
- Actions are recorded in an audit log that the database itself prevents from being edited or deleted

**Application**
- A content security policy limits what the app's pages can load and run
- Uploaded files are checked for type and size
- Messages from our payment provider are verified by signature
- Secrets are kept on our servers, and automated checks keep them out of code sent to the browser

**Backups**
- The database is backed up daily by our database provider. Uploaded files are held in the provider's file storage and are not part of the database backup

## Annex 3: Subprocessors

- **Supabase**: database, sign-in and file storage. Handles all Customer Personal Data. United States
- **Render**: hosting for the application. Handles Customer Personal Data as it is processed. United States. Uses Cloudflare's network in front of its services
- **OpenAI**: reading photos and PDFs, and matching spreadsheet columns. Handles uploaded files, and spreadsheet headings and sample rows. United States
- **Postmark**: sending email. Handles recipient names and email addresses, team member names, requirement names and upload links. United States
- **PostHog**: usage and error reports. Handles random account and organization IDs and event counts. It receives no team member data. United States

**Lemon Squeezy** is the merchant of record for payments. It handles payment details as a Controller under its own terms and does not receive Customer Personal Data from us. It is listed here for completeness.
