CertMatrix helps you keep track of your team's certifications, licenses and training records. It reads photos of cards to suggest details, shows who has what on one screen, sends reminder emails before a date passes, and produces exports of your records.

> **CertMatrix tracks dates and sends reminders. It does not determine legal compliance.**

CertMatrix does not decide whether you, your team or any person meets any law, regulation, contract, license condition or insurance requirement. You remain responsible for:

- Knowing which requirements apply to your business and your people
- Checking that the information in CertMatrix is correct, including anything read from a photo
- Acting on reminders, and not relying on a reminder as your only safeguard
- Keeping any records the law requires you to keep, for as long as it requires

A status such as "In date" reflects only the dates that were entered. CertMatrix does not check with the issuer that a card is genuine or still valid. Reminders are sent by email, and email can fail or arrive late. Exports such as the audit binder are a copy of your records. They are not a certification of compliance.

## 6. AI Features

When you or a Team Member uploads a photo or PDF, we send that file to OpenAI to read details such as the name, the title, the issuer and the dates. We send the file itself and nothing else about your Organization.

What comes back is a suggestion. One of two things then happens:

- A person in your Organization reviews the suggestion next to the photo and confirms or corrects it
- For a clean upload made through a Team Member's own upload link, CertMatrix may file it automatically. An automatic filing is recorded in the history and can be undone

AI reading can be wrong. Dates, names and numbers may be misread. You are responsible for checking them.

When you import a spreadsheet, CertMatrix may send the column headings, a few sample rows and your requirement names to OpenAI to work out which column is which.

Our [Privacy Policy](/apps/certmatrix/privacy) explains how long OpenAI can keep what we send.
